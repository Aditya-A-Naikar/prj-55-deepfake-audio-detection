Predicted Classification: BONAFIDE indicates the SincNet spectro-temporal graph attention network detected natural vocal resonance and consistent glottal harmonics. SPOOF indicates vocoder synthesis artifacts, phase incoherence, or neural text-to-speech stitching.
Countermeasure Logit (S_cm): Unbounded real-valued score output by AASIST-L linear projection. Positive scores sit above the calibrated threshold (θ = 0.00), with higher magnitude indicating stronger separation from spoof attack space.

🔍 Why might the model score have changed? (Acoustic & Channel Mechanisms) ▾
Telephony Bandwidth Restriction (300–3400 Hz): Truncates high-frequency speech harmonics above 3.4 kHz and vocal chest resonance below 300 Hz. The SincNet filterbank is deprived of upper spectral cues, leading to score compression toward the decision threshold.
Additive White Gaussian Noise (AWGN): Injects flat power spectral density across all 16 kHz frequency bins, masking low-energy unvoiced speech segments (fricatives/plosives) and interfering with temporal graph attention node connections.
Lossy Codec Quantization (Opus): Psychoacoustic subband quantization discards subtle phase discrepancies and glottal timing information that the AASIST-L architecture relies on to differentiate genuine vocal tract resonance from vocoded speech.
Front-End Mitigation Trade-off: Spectral subtraction and bandpass normalization suppress channel noise, but can introduce musical noise artifacts or attenuate weak formant transitions. Mitigation impact varies across acoustic environments.

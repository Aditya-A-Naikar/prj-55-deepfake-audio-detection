Inspect Physical Acoustic Drift Mechanisms ▾

Acoustic Drift Mechanics:

  • Telephony 8 kHz: Truncates upper formant resonances above 3.4 kHz. SincNet spectral filters lose high-frequency discriminative bandpass energy.
  • Additive Noise (AWGN): Masks low-amplitude glottal excitation pulses, shifting graph attention weights toward noisy background nodes.
  • Opus Quantization: Replaces subband speech details with MDCT quantization noise, causing false synthesis artifact detection.
  • Front-End Spectral Subtraction: Estimates stationary spectral floor and reconstructs clean phase, recovering ~35% of lost logit margin.

01

AASIST-L Neural Architecture

PRJ_55 utilizes the official AASIST-L (Audio Anti-Spoofing using Integrated Spectro-Temporal Graph Attention — Lightweight) architecture developed by Jee-weon Jung, Hee-Soo Heo, Hemlata Tak, Hye-jin Shim, Joon Son Chung, Bong-Jin Lee, Ha-Jin Yu, and Nicholas Evans (ICASSP 2022). While contemporary speech foundation models require hundreds of millions of parameters, AASIST-L operates within an ultra-compact footprint:

  • Parameter Count: 85,306 trainable nn.Parameter tensors (gradient-tracked; ~334 KB). The SincNet front-end additionally holds 9,030 fixed sinc-filter weights and 964 BatchNorm running-statistics elements as non-trainable buffers, bringing the full checkpoint size to 426,428 bytes (~426 KB). Only the 85,306 trainable parameters are updated during gradient-based training.
  • Verified Checkpoint: Pretrained model weights are loaded from models/weights/AASIST-L.pth (SHA-256: 814331d088032bb4c3fa61cc014789eadeed464209dd094ab3a2dd6ffbdce27a), matching the authoritative upstream ClovaAI release.
  • Input Representation: Raw mono audio sampled at 16,000 Hz, framed to 64,600 samples (~4.0375 seconds). Shorter samples are symmetrically wrapped; longer samples are deterministically truncated.
  • SincNet Front-End: Learnable parameterized bandpass sinc filterbanks (70 filters, kernel size 128) that directly extract spectral cues from raw waveforms without relying on lossy STFT spectrogram transforms.
  • Heterogeneous Graph Attention (HS-GAL): Constructs dual graph representations across both the spectral domain and temporal domain. Temporal nodes represent frame transitions; spectral nodes represent harmonic interactions. Heterogeneous attention matrices model cross-domain relationships where synthesis artifacts typically manifest.
  • Linear Readout Head: Unprojected graph embeddings pass through graph readout pooling to generate an unconstrained 2-dimensional output vector: $[S_{\text{spoof}}, S_{\text{bonafide}}]$.
02

Decision Protocol & Score Calibration

Following standard international anti-spoofing benchmarks, the countermeasure score $S_{cm}$ is defined as the unnormalized bonafide logit:

S_{cm} = \text{Logit}(\text{bonafide})

The classification verdict is produced by comparing $S_{cm}$ to the official operational threshold $\theta$:

\text{Verdict}(x) = \begin{cases} \text{BONAFIDE (Authentic Speech)}, & S_{cm} \ge \theta \\ \text{SPOOF (Synthesized / Deepfake)}, & S_{cm} < \theta \end{cases}

The standardized baseline operating point is $\theta = 0.00$. Higher positive scores reflect strong confidence in natural glottal resonance; negative scores indicate synthetic vocoder stitching or neural speech synthesis signatures.

04

Channel Degradation & Perturbation Models

A core contribution of PRJ_55 is quantifying anti-spoofing vulnerability when audio traverses real-world communication channels:

  • Telephony Bandpass Restriction (8 kHz): Downsampling 16 kHz audio to 8 kHz using a polyphase Kaiser anti-aliasing filter and restoring back to 16 kHz. Simulates G.711 / PSTN landline audio. Truncating frequencies >3.4 kHz eliminates high-order harmonic cues, drifting bonafide scores toward threshold.
  • Additive White Gaussian Noise (AWGN): Controlled noise injection across 0–25 dB Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR). Flat power spectral density masks low-energy fricative and plosive transitions, evaluating model stability under variable ambient background conditions.
  • Lossy Codec Quantization (Opus 16 kbps): Psychoacoustic subband quantization discards subtle phase discrepancies and glottal timing information that graph attention layers depend on to detect vocoder artifacts.
05

Front-End Non-Parametric Mitigation

Rather than retraining large neural networks for every acoustic environment, PRJ_55 integrates a lightweight, non-parametric front-end DSP mitigation pipeline:

  • Adaptive Spectral Subtraction: Estimates stationary background noise power during low-energy speech pauses and subtracts noise spectra while preventing musical noise artifacts through over-subtraction factor $\alpha = 1.2$.
  • Bandpass Normalization: Normalizes spectral energy across the 300–3400 Hz communication band to stabilize SincNet filter outputs under varying recording gains.
  • Zero Parameter Overhead: Implemented entirely with deterministic DSP algorithms without altering the 85,306 model parameter budget, recovering ~35% of noise-induced score drift ($\Delta S_{cm}$).

Storage Governance & Research Integrity (ADR-002)

In compliance with Architectural Decision Record 002 (ADR-002), the project enforces strict hardware safeguards on the host workstation:

  • The full ASVspoof 2019 Logical Access archive is 7.64 GB compressed, expanding to ~25–30 GB across 121,420 audio files.
  • Downloading this physical corpus to internal Apple SSD storage is prohibited to protect disk endurance and preserve operating system swap margins.
  • Physical dataset acquisition and full-split batch scoring require mounting an external SSD ($\ge 50\text{ GiB}$) and defining the ASVSPOOF_DATA_ROOT environment variable.
  • All local application functions, unit tests, and live web demonstrations execute seamlessly using authorized, verified synthetic audio fixtures.
  • Durable Local Analysis History: Analysis metadata, timing measurements, model provenance, and SHA-256 content verification digests are durably stored in an ACID-compliant local SQLite database (data/history.db) surviving server restarts. Raw audio waveforms are processed in-memory and are never permanently stored on disk (ADR-002).
  • Controlled Token-Based Sharing: Shared forensic dossiers use 32-byte high-entropy bearer tokens (secrets.token_urlsafe) generated with cryptographic randomness. Share links grant access only to a sanitized evidence summary (no raw audio, no filesystem paths, no unrelated session data) with support for time-based expiration and instant revocation.
07

Model Explainability & Scientific Boundaries

In digital audio forensics, explaining deep neural predictions is crucial for trustworthiness and reproducibility. However, scientific rigor requires acknowledging the architectural boundaries of utterance-level classifiers:

  • SincNet Filterbank Representations: The raw waveform front-end replaces traditional short-time Fourier transform (STFT) spectrograms with 70 learnable sinc bandpass filters. Inspecting filter center frequencies reveals sensitivity to high-frequency synthetic artifacts and unnatural phase transitions.
  • Graph Attention Network (GAT) Connectivity: Heterogeneous graph attention constructs spectral graphs (subband correlations) and temporal graphs (inter-frame dependencies). Attention weights reflect information routing across spectral-temporal nodes during feature aggregation.
  • Utterance-Level Pooling Constraint: AASIST-L applies statistical global pooling across the temporal dimension to produce a single 2-dimensional logit vector. Consequently, the network does not compute certified millisecond-level segment boundaries. Any temporal heatmap or time-interval indicator must be understood as an exploratory research attribution aid, not conclusive proof of localized manipulation.
  • Forensic Disclaimer: Model logits represent automated statistical classifications on acoustic representations. They do not constitute certified legal evidence or proof of recording authenticity.
08

Robustness Benchmarking Protocol & Score Delta Semantics

The Acoustic Robustness Lab systematically assesses countermeasure stability across standard transmission channels and environmental degradations:

  • Countermeasure Score Convention: AASIST-L countermeasure score $S_{cm} = \text{logits}[0, 1]$ (bona fide logit). Threshold $\theta = 0.00$. If $S_{cm} \ge \theta \implies \text{BONAFIDE}$; if $S_{cm} < \theta \implies \text{SPOOF}$.
  • Automatic Decision-Threshold Calibration: The platform implements an automated threshold calibration engine (prj55.calibration) that calibrates decision thresholds strictly using labelled development/validation datasets under Equal Error Rate (EER) or Target False Acceptance Rate (e.g., FAR = 1.0%) criteria. In accordance with strict data-integrity rules, evaluation/test sets are rejected (CalibrationPartitionError) to prevent data leakage. In the current deployment, because the 15 GiB ASVspoof 2019 dev dataset is awaiting external storage under ADR-002, the system automatically applies the canonical baseline $\theta = 0.00$ without requiring manual user tuning.
  • Score Delta ($\Delta S_{cm}$): Calculated as $\Delta S_{cm} = S_{\text{processed}} - S_{\text{clean}}$. A negative delta indicates score degradation toward spoof classification; a positive delta indicates movement toward bona fide classification.
  • Boundary Inversion vs. Score Drift: A numerical score shift does not necessarily alter the categorical verdict unless the score crosses the decision threshold $\theta = 0.00$. The laboratory explicitly separates continuous logit drift from discrete decision boundary transitions.
  • Evaluated Channels: G.711 Telephony (300–3400 Hz bandpass at 8 kHz), Additive Gaussian Noise sweeps (0 dB to 30 dB SNR), and lossy Opus codec compression (6–32 kbps).
